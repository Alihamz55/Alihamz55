# Potato Phone vs. Game Too Big 6 (English, American Gen Z slang)

**Adapted from:** `naskah/asep-maksa-game-berat.md` (Asep Maksain Game Berat di HP Kentang).
**Channel:** new English-language channel (for viewers outside Indonesia).
**Adaptation notes (versi Indonesia → Inggris):**
- **"HP kentang" → "potato phone".** Istilah ini memang sudah dipakai di internet bahasa Inggris, jadi langsung dipahami.
- **Game fiktif "GTB 6"** dibaca "**Game Too Big 6**" (plesetan baru yang tetap aman dari merek).
- **Pak Darman dan Ibu jadi "Dad" dan "Mom".** Asep tetap Asep, supaya tokohnya sama di dua channel.
- **Plesetan tambahan khas Gen Z vs bapak kolot:** Asep pakai slang ("no cap", "bussin", "lowkey", "W/L"), lalu Dad salah paham (*"cap"* dikira topi). Ini setara dengan Pak Darman yang salah paham bahasa gaul di versi Indonesia.
- **Tanpa merek:** tidak menyebut nama game asli, merek HP, atau aplikasi presentasi.
- **Caption:** "Comedy skit. All characters are fictional. Don't overheat your phone!"

**Voices (Speko/Gemini, English):**
- **Asep:** Achird. Instruction: "Hyped American Gen Z teen, overconfident, talks fast, uses slang naturally, funny."
- **Dad:** Charon. Instruction: "Old-school American dad, deadpan, confused by slang."
- **Mom:** Gacrux. Instruction: "Loud, practical American mom, nagging but funny."

**Costumes:** same as the Indonesian version (`asepGamer` → afro + soot after the explosion, `darmanSarung`, `ibuRoll`). Visuals stay the same; on-screen text is re-rendered in English.

## 2. Hook (0–3 sec)
*(Close-up of the phone screen: "GAME TOO BIG 6 — LOADING 1%". Asep stares, eyes wide.)*

## 3. Full script

**Asep:** Game Too Big 6 just dropped! And I'm running it... on a potato phone!

*(Phone screen: "STORAGE FULL".)*

**Asep:** Storage full? Easy. Delete my ex's pics... delete the diet app... delete Mom and Dad's wedding photos.

**Dad:** *(from the doorway)* ASEP!

*(Banner: "DOWNLOADING 150 GB". Time skip. The phone glows red and starts smoking a little.)*

**Asep:** Bro, it's hot! Into the fridge it goes.

**Mom:** Asep! That fridge is for my fish, not your phone!

*(Asep plays. The game moves one frame per second.)*

**Asep:** This game is bussin, no cap!

**Dad:** Why would you need a cap? You're indoors.

**Dad:** And son... is that a game, or a slideshow?

**Asep:** Dad, the graphics are so realistic... there's even smoke!

*(Phone hisses... POOF! Thick smoke. Asep now has an afro and a sooty face. Beat.)*

**Asep:** ...Told you, Mom. The explosion's in 4K.

**Mom:** The fridge smells like burnt phone!

**Dad:** *(hands over an ancient phone)* Here. Use mine. It's got Snake.

*(Asep plays Snake. Close-up on his emotional face.)*

**Asep:** These graphics... lowkey insane.

*(End card: "POTATO PHONE 0 – GAME TOO BIG 1")*

## 4. Main punchline
**"Dad, the graphics are so realistic... there's even smoke!"** Asep is proud of the smoke, which is actually his phone about to blow up. Then **POOF!** Camera kick and `fxJudge` "POOF!".

## 5. Tags
- **"Why would you need a cap? You're indoors."** Dad takes the slang literally (the classic Gen Z vs. old dad joke).
- **"...Told you, Mom. The explosion's in 4K."** Still overconfident, even with an afro.
- **"The fridge smells like burnt phone!"** Mom only cares about her fridge.

## 6. Closing callback
Asep plays Snake on Dad's ancient phone and says, emotionally: **"These graphics... lowkey insane."** This calls back to "the graphics are so realistic".

## 7. Estimated length
About 44–48 seconds at `atempo=1.12` with 0.15–0.3 sec pauses, plus the 6-second end card (English version). Total under 55 seconds.

## Terjemahan singkat (untuk pengecekan)
- "Bussin, no cap" = "mantap, serius/nggak bohong". Dad salah paham "cap" sebagai topi: "Ngapain pakai topi? Kamu kan di dalam rumah."
- "Lowkey insane" = "diam-diam keren banget".
- "Slideshow" = "slide presentasi" (patah-patah).
- "In 4K" = "kualitas HD" (versi Indonesia: "Ledakannya HD").
